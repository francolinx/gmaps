GMAPS airport-graph schema 1.0.0

SCOPE
This is a source-grounded topology-draft interchange. Its purpose is to preserve
what the diagram shows without creating surveyed geometry or authorizations.
The v1 schema intentionally requires geometry and segment dimensions to be null,
and automatic routing / metric prediction to remain false. Preserve the draft
when adding a future reviewed geometry model under a new schema version.

IDENTIFIERS
All entity IDs are namespaced by airport, entity type, and stable name:
KLGA:TWY:D, KLGA:RWY:04-22, KLGA:END:04,
KLGA:N:D_RWY04-22, KLGA:S:D_AA_TO_RWY.
A runway surface has two reciprocal direction records. Taxiway names, named
areas, runway ends and graph nodes are different namespaces. IDs are stable keys,
not coordinates. Do not merge airports or rename existing keys without a mapping.

COLLECTIONS
sources: original provenance, effective period, retrieval date, source scope,
local path and SHA-256. Date eligibility is only the printed period as retrieved.
surfaces: physical runway pairs, taxiways and selected named areas. Whole-runway
published dimensions are in feet and do not define a centerline or segment length.
runway_ends: directional identifiers, reciprocal links, aliases and parent runway.
nodes: evidenced pavement junctions. No node currently asserts a hold position.
segments: visible links along a named surface, bounded by the listed junctions.
The from/to order is storage order. Operational direction remains unknown.
Atomicity is junctions_only_hold_boundaries_unresolved: a reviewed implementation
must split links at verified hold/control boundaries before they become routable.
hold_points: separate approach-specific constraints or charted hold annotations.
node_id, offset and geometry remain null. Physical_marking_status distinguishes
not_located conceptual records from chart_depicted_location_unmeasured records.
zones: symbolic conflict references and hotspot annotations. Brown hotspot
ellipses are not runway-occupancy polygons; none is usable for occupancy math.
route_templates: ordered draft walks, or unresolved surface words without a walk.
These are illustrative graph paths, not historical or current ATC permissions.
issues: per-airport review queue with affected IDs and required evidence.

EVIDENCE AND UNCERTAINTY
Every entity carries evidence[], review and uncertainty. Evidence identifies a
source, one-based PDF page (or null for a text reference), human-readable locator,
claim and method. A page locator is not metric geometry. Design_convention marks
an engineering constraint rather than a fact printed on the chart.
Uncertainty.label is source_supported, ambiguous, unknown or design_only;
reason and alternatives preserve the unresolved question. Confidence is null
because no calibrated extraction probabilities were measured. Source_supported
means the cited claim is visible in the source, not expert-approved or current.
Review defaults to pending; acceptance requires reviewer and reviewed_at.

VALIDATION RULES
1. Parse strict JSON; validate against the supplied Draft 2020-12 schema. Unknown
   keys are rejected. Empty arrays mean unmodeled data, not a complete empty field.
2. IDs must be unique and airport-scoped. Every graph/source/route reference must
   exist. Never link a KBOS entity to KLGA data.
3. Runway end numbers and suffixes must be reciprocal, with exactly two ends per
   physical runway. Preserve 04 vs 04L/04R and 33L vs 33R.
4. Every segment endpoint must contain the segment's surface. A route's node and
   segment sequences must make a continuous walk in the stated surface order.
   Storage-order reversal in a draft does not imply permission in either direction.
5. No fuzzy identifier correction without preserving candidates and uncertainty.
   Duplicate letters and numeric suffixes are meaningful. Unknown-in-inventory
   is not proof that an identifier does not exist at the airport.
6. Hold approaches must attach to the correct segment/side. Do not place a hold
   at the runway-intersection node or reuse the same point for both approaches.
   Requests/readbacks, blank hold fields and chart connectivity grant no authority.
7. Never synthesize coordinates, speeds, lengths, turn radii, protected widths,
   holding offsets or polygon boundaries. PDF pixels and printed label positions
   are evidence locators only. A chart grid is not a validated georeference.
8. Require date order, local source integrity and preserved provenance. Keep chart
   edition, revision mark, retrieval time, historical applicability and live
   status separate. Do not silently merge graphs from different chart cycles.
9. Accepted reviews need a named reviewer and timestamp. A schema pass alone
   does not accept topology; version and reviewer evidence must survive promotion.
10. Missing motion/geometry cannot yield a precise position or a negative conflict
    result. Predictions need fresh tracks, identity association, shared clocks,
    uncertainty, footprints, modeled route hypotheses and declared dynamics.

AUTOMATED VS HUMAN CHECKS
validate.py enforces the file schema, ID/reference integrity, reciprocal runways,
route walks, hold linkage, recorded review identity, date order and source hashes.
Its resolver demonstrates exact identifier lookup and missing-suffix candidates.
The four speech/state examples in resolution_examples.json are integration
acceptance fixtures; no full ASR/parser/state machine is implemented or tested.
Visual correctness, labels confused with symbols, missed branches, current
restrictions and physical accuracy require independent review. Programmatic
connectivity cannot prove that a line was read correctly from a PDF.

PROMOTION CONTRACT
After expert review, create a new geometry-capable schema and derived dataset;
keep v1 files immutable as the reviewed input. Record CRS (or SIM-local frame),
units, measurement method, source control points and residual/accuracy bounds;
separate chart depiction from measured physical truth. Split at hold/zone entry
and exit boundaries, add movement restrictions and explicit authorized turns.
Validate path continuity and source versions again. Add metric occupancy tests
using independent tracks and hidden expected answers; do not derive timing from
historical media captions or use a whole-runway length as an approach distance.
