How to read this page

  • Left is the radio as it is today: the tower talks, pilots and drivers read back. Nothing checks one clearance against another.
  • Middle is the same radio with GMAPS: it types each call, keeps a ledger of who is cleared to do what on which runway, and when two open clearances need the same runway it says so, names both, and cites the rule.
  • Right shows where things are on a fictional airport, from simulated position reports. The look-ahead only speaks when it has fresh positions; otherwise it says unavailable, never “clear”.
  • The time ruler at the bottom marks the moments: when GMAPS warns, and when the controller steps in. In the fictional scenarios both times are in the script.

GMAPS is decision support. It does not transmit, it does not move anything, and the controller decides. Every warning means: two recorded authorizations, or an open obligation, at that moment.
