About this view

Layout is real, traffic is not. Runways, taxiways, aprons and hold bars are LaGuardia from OpenStreetMap, placed in the FAA airport-diagram frame. The aircraft (SimAir 212) and the vehicle (Rescue 7) are the fictional scripted GMAPS scenarios, not any real event.

Positions are simulated. The look-ahead is computed on the fictional SIM-1 model and drawn here. The ledger runs on LaGuardia identifiers (runway 04/22, taxiway G). Decision support only: GMAPS never transmits, and the controller decides.
